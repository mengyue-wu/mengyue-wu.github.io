Profile & Mission

About Me

I am an Associate Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor in the School of Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), and a core faculty member of the X-LANCE Lab. I earned my Ph.D. from the University of Melbourne (2017) and my B.Sc./B.A. from Beijing Normal University (2011).

My research centers on Digital Mental Health—bridging computational linguistics, speech science, clinical psychiatry, and AI ethics. Rather than reducing human psychology to black-box classification labels, our group develops interpretable mental state representations, clinically grounded diagnostic dialogue agents, and rigorous safety benchmarks for trustworthy healthcare AI.

Digital Mental Health · Core Pillars

Research Directions & Conceptual Frameworks

Fig 1. Interpretable Multi-Disorder & Cognitive State Representation
Pillar 01 · Representation

Computational Mental State Representation

Moving beyond coarse binary labels to clinically interpretable narratives—modeling fine-grained psychiatric symptoms (PsySym, MATCHA), stressful life events, self-supervised acoustic embeddings (DEPA), and semantic verbal fluency markers for dementia screening.

Representative Works: Findings of EMNLP 2026 (MATCHA), npj Dementia 2026, Findings of ACL 2026, EMNLP 2022.
Fig 2. Clinical-Grounded Diagnostic Dialogue & Comorbidity Reasoning
Pillar 02 · Clinical Reasoning

Clinical AI & Mental State Reasoning

Developing clinically grounded dialogue systems that actively infer latent mental states through structured psychiatric interviewing (SCID protocols), psychiatric comorbidity modeling from real medical records, longitudinal causal mapping, and realistic patient simulation.

Representative Works: ICLR 2026 (Comorbidity Dialogues), ACL 2025, NAACL 2024, EMNLP 2023 (PsyEx), EMNLP 2022 (D4).
Fig 3. Multi-Dimensional Psychological & Emotional Intelligence Evaluation
Pillar 03 · Evaluation

Psychological Intelligence & LLM Benchmarking

Systematically evaluating how foundation models perceive, reason about, and respond to human psychological states—uncovering fragmentation across emotional perception, cognitive appraisal, and multi-turn therapeutic interaction.

Representative Works: npj Mental Health Research 2026 (PsyEval), arXiv 2026 (Emotional Intelligence Fragmentation).
Fig 4. Situating Vulnerability & Medical Ethics Alignment Pipeline
Pillar 04 · AI Safety & Ethics

Ethical Governance & Safety Alignment

Addressing relational dependency and user vulnerability in anthropomorphic AI—establishing human-centric medical ethics alignment pipelines (MedEthicEval) and multi-dimensional crisis intervention guardrails for real-world clinical systems.

Representative Works: Situating Vulnerability (2026), AAAI 2026 (Medical Ethics Alignment), NAACL 2025 (MedEthicEval).
