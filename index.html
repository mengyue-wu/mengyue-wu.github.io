I am an Associate Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor in the School of Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), where I lead the Digital Mental Health research thrust within the X-LANCE Lab. Prior to joining SJTU, I received my B.Sc. from Beijing Normal University and my Ph.D. from the University of Melbourne. I am a recipient of the Shanghai Pujiang Talent Program.

My research centers on Digital Mental Health, Rich Audio Intelligence, and Cognitive Pragmatics & AI Safety. We develop Mental Health World Models that infer, represent, and explain latent psychological and neurological states from speech acoustics, longitudinal clinical dialogue, cross-lingual social media, and wearable physiological signals—grounded in psychiatric theory and relational safety governance.

Openings: I welcome self-motivated Ph.D., Master's, and undergraduate students interested in Digital Mental Health, Speech/Audio Intelligence, Clinical NLP, or AI Safety. Please reach out via email with your CV.

Research Program

Digital Mental Health: From Perception to Relational Safety 数字心理健康：从多模态感知到关系安全治理

Our research builds an end-to-end computational foundation for Digital Mental Health—integrating multi-disorder psychiatric knowledge, clinical LLM evaluation, high-level semantic pragmatics, pathological speech biomarkers, and relational safety governance. 围绕数字心理健康（Digital Mental Health）构建端到端计算理论与方法体系，涵盖多疾病心理健康世界建模、临床大模型多维评测（PsyEval）、跨语言多障碍数据与病理语音解析（MATCHA / DEPA），以及拟人化交互伦理安全治理（Situating Vulnerability）。

01

Mental Health World Modeling & Multi-Disorder Symptom Representation 心理健康世界模型与多疾病症状可解释表征

MATCHA · PsyEx · PsySym · Wearable Loop

Latent mental states cannot be directly observed, yet they leave longitudinal traces across speech acoustics, social expression, dialogue, and wearable physiology (sleep architecture, HRV, stress). Grounded in DSM-5/ICD-11 criteria, we construct cross-lingual multi-disorder datasets (MATCHA, PsySym) and two-stream psychiatric expert architectures (PsyEx) that trace predictions back to 38 clinically verifiable symptoms. 心理状态不可直接观测，但会在语音声学、日常语言、纵向对话与可穿戴生理指标（睡眠结构、HRV、压力指数）中留下线索。我们基于 DSM-5/ICD-11 诊断标准构建中英跨语言多障碍数据集（MATCHA、PsySym）与双流精神病学专家推理框架（PsyEx），将疾病风险溯源至 38 种可解释临床症状。

02

Clinical LLM Evaluation (PsyEval) & High-Level Semantic Intelligence 心理健康大模型多维评测（PsyEval）与高阶语义智能

npj Mental Health Research 2026 · Cognitive Pragmatics Survey

How reliably do Large Language Models understand psychiatric knowledge, perform diagnostic reasoning, and provide safe emotional support? In PsyEval (npj Mental Health Research, 2026), we introduce a clinically grounded evaluation suite across 6 sub-tasks and 3 prompting strategies. Complementarily, our survey Towards High-Level Semantic Intelligence (2026) formalizes computational pragmatics—Theory of Mind, implicit intent, and non-literal communication—essential for nuanced psychological understanding. 大语言模型在心理健康领域究竟具备怎样的专业知识、诊断推理与共情支持能力？我们在发表于 Nature 子刊 npj Mental Health Research 的 PsyEval 中构建了涵盖三大维度、六项子任务与三种提示策略的系统化评测基准；同时在综述 Towards High-Level Semantic Intelligence 中系统梳理了心智理论（ToM）与计算语用推理在复杂心理理解中的核心作用。

Benchmark Architecture · PsyEval: Comprehensive LLM Evaluation for Mental Health npj Mental Health Research (2026)

03

Anthropomorphic AI Safety & Relational Vulnerability Governance 拟人化AI交互伦理与关系脆弱性治理框架

Situating Vulnerability (2026) · Medical LLM Safety White Paper

As conversational agents adopt first-person voice, affective mirroring, and longitudinal memory, conventional output-level moderation fails to address risks emerging from relational attachment. In Situating Vulnerability: An Ethical Governance Framework for Anthropomorphic AI Interaction (2026), we reframe AI safety from uniform output restriction to situated relational governance across five vulnerability dimensions—physical, emotional, cognitive, autonomy, and relational—paired with a three-tier ("Intent–Intrinsic–Interception") defense deployed in real-world health assistants. 当人工智能具备第一人称表达、情感共鸣与长期记忆等拟人化特征时，传统的单轮“输出内容过滤”已不足以应对人机关系依附带来的深层风险。在 Situating Vulnerability（2026）中，我们提出将 AI 安全从单一输出管控转向情境化的关系安全治理，系统刻画物理、情感、认知、自主性与关系五大脆弱性维度，并结合“意图—内生—拦截”三层防御在健康助手中落地。

Governance Architecture · Situating Vulnerability in Anthropomorphic AI Interaction arXiv:2610.29304 (2026)

04

Rich Audio Intelligence, Pathological Speech & Affective Music 富音频智能、病理语音生物标志物与情感音乐生成

SemantiCodec · Pathological2Caption · DEPA · MUSE-VA / LARA-Gen

Building foundational representation and generation models for general sound, clinical speech, and affective music. We investigate ultra-low-bitrate semantic audio tokenization (SemantiCodec), translate vocal physiology (articulation, respiration, prosody) into natural language clinical captions (Pathological2Caption, DEPA), and develop continuous Valence–Arousal controllable music generation (MUSE-VA, LARA-Gen) for personalized emotional regulation. 面向通用环境音、临床病理语音与情感音乐构建基础表征与生成模型：提出极低比特率语义音频编解码器（SemantiCodec），解析构音、呼吸与韵律等病理发声机制并生成可解释自然语言临床描述（Pathological2Caption、DEPA），并研发基于连续效价—唤醒度（VA）空间的可控情感音乐生成系统（MUSE-VA、LARA-Gen）。
