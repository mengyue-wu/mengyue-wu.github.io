About Me

Biography & Vision

I am an Associate Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor in the School of Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), and a core faculty member of the X-LANCE Lab. Before joining SJTU in 2018, I earned my Ph.D. from the University of Melbourne (2017) and my B.Sc./B.A. from Beijing Normal University (2011).

My research focuses on Digital Mental Health—bridging computational linguistics, speech science, clinical psychiatry, and AI ethics. Rather than treating mental health assessment as a black-box classification task, our group investigates how latent psychological states manifest across multimodal signals and longitudinal interactions, and how foundation models can reason, converse, and intervene safely.

💡 Prospective Students & Collaborators: I am always looking for self-motivated Ph.D., Master's, and undergraduate students interested in Digital Mental Health, Clinical Dialogue Systems, and AI Safety. Feel free to reach out via email!

Research Directions · Digital Mental Health

Three Core Pillars
PILLAR 01

Computational Mental State Representation

Moving beyond coarse binary labels to clinically interpretable narratives—modeling fine-grained psychiatric symptoms (PsySym), stressful life events (PsyEvent), pathological speech biomarkers, and cognitive markers for dementia screening.

PsySym / MATCHA Pathological Speech Dementia Screening
PILLAR 02

Clinical AI & Mental State Reasoning

Developing clinically grounded dialogue agents that actively infer latent mental states through structured psychiatric interviewing, comorbidity reasoning, longitudinal causal mapping, and realistic patient digital twins.

SCID Interviewing Comorbidity Causal Mapping
PILLAR 03

Trustworthy AI for Mental Health

Establishing rigorous evaluation benchmarks (PsyEval, MedEthicEval), ethical governance frameworks for anthropomorphic AI vulnerability, and multi-layered safety guardrails for real-world clinical deployment.

PsyEval AI Governance Medical Ethics

Mental Health World Modeling: Closed-Loop Architecture

Perception → Knowledge-Grounded Reasoning → Safe Interaction
