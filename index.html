About

I am an Associate Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor in the School of Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), and a faculty member of the X-LANCE Lab. I received my B.Sc./B.A. from Beijing Normal University and my Ph.D. from the University of Melbourne.

My research centers on Digital Mental Health—uncovering the computational principles underlying human mental states through speech, language, and clinical interaction, and building clinically grounded, trustworthy AI systems for mental healthcare.

Research Directions · Digital Mental Health

1. Computational Mental State Representation

Developing interpretable computational representations of latent mental states from speech and language—progressing from binary disease labels to clinically grounded narratives, symptom modeling (PsySym), stressful life event tracking (PsyEvent), and cognitive markers for dementia screening.

2. Clinical AI & Mental State Reasoning

Designing clinically grounded dialogue systems that actively infer mental states through structured diagnostic interviewing (SCID protocols), psychiatric comorbidity modeling, patient simulation, and longitudinal causal reasoning.

3. Trustworthy AI for Mental Health

Advancing ethical alignment, psychological intelligence evaluation (PsyEval, MedEthicEval), vulnerability governance in anthropomorphic AI, and safe clinical deployment.
