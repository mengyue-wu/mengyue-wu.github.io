About Me

I am an Associate Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor in the School of Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), and a core faculty member of the X-LANCE Lab. I received my B.Sc./B.A. from Beijing Normal University and my Ph.D. from the University of Melbourne.

My research focuses on Digital Mental Health—bridging computational linguistics, speech science, clinical psychiatry, and AI ethics. We investigate how latent psychological states manifest across multimodal signals and longitudinal interactions, and how foundation models can reason, converse, and intervene safely.

Research Directions · Digital Mental Health

1. Mental State Representation

Developing interpretable computational representations from speech and language—progressing from binary disease labels to fine-grained psychiatric symptoms (PsySym), life events, and cognitive markers for dementia screening.

2. Clinical AI & Reasoning

Designing clinically grounded dialogue systems that actively infer mental states through structured diagnostic interviewing (SCID protocols), psychiatric comorbidity reasoning, and patient simulation.

3. Trustworthy Mental AI

Advancing rigorous evaluation benchmarks (PsyEval, MedEthicEval), ethical governance for anthropomorphic AI vulnerability, and safe clinical alignment for real-world deployment.
