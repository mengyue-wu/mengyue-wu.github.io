About Me

I am an Associate Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor in the School of Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and a member of the X-LANCE Lab. I received my Bachelor's degree from Beijing Normal University and my Ph.D. from the University of Melbourne.

My primary research focus lies in Digital Mental Health, spanning large language models for mental health, proactive dialogue systems, psychiatric evaluation benchmarks, and ethical AI safety governance.

Research Directions

Our research centers on Digital Mental Health, addressing core challenges in computational mental science and trustworthy healthcare AI:

  • Digital Mental Health & World Models: Constructing computational models to represent, track, and interpret mental health states over time using multi-source interactions.
  • Proactive & Empathetic Dialogue Systems: Developing interactive psychiatric interviewers and support agents with aligned clinical reasoning and controllable empathy.
  • Evaluation Benchmarks & Datasets: Building rigorous benchmarks (such as PsyEval, MATCHA, and CogBench) for psychological assessment and mental health large language models.
  • AI Ethics, Safety & Governance: Establishing ethical safety alignment frameworks and risk-mitigation strategies for high-risk psychological and medical AI applications.
