关于我们与研究愿景About Research Vision

我目前担任上海交通大学计算机学院副教授、博士生导师，是机器智能所与跨媒体语言智能实验室（X-LANCE）核心成员。长期聚焦音频信号处理、自然语言处理、计算精神病学、心理健康大模型与情感计算。 I am an Associate Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor in the School of Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, affiliated with the X-LANCE Lab. My research focuses on audio processing, natural language processing, computational mental health, and affective computing.

心理状态本身不可直接观测，却会在语音、语言、可穿戴生理数据（如睡眠、压力指数）与持续交互中留下线索。我们的研究目标是建立从可观测信号到心理健康状态的闭环世界模型，连接多模态感知、知识增强推理、主动访谈、用户模拟与情感生成。 Mental states are unobservable directly but leave traces in speech, language, wearables (sleep, stress), and interactions. Our goal is to build closed-loop world models linking multimodal perception, knowledge-enhanced reasoning, active interviewing, simulation, and affective generation.

Pillar 01 · Representation

心理状态表征与感知Mental State Representation & Perception

突破单一诊断标签，深度解析发声生理机制（构音、韵律、呼吸控制）、社交图文及可穿戴生理指标（睡眠、压力），建立跨疾病、可解释的症状与认知表征。Moving beyond binary labels to parse speech physiology, social logs, and wearable metrics (sleep/stress) for interpretable symptom and cognitive representations.

代表成果：EMNLP (MATCHA), npj Dementia, ACL (Pathological2Caption)
图 2. 临床知识引导的主动诊断对话与因果推理Fig 2. Clinical-Grounded Diagnostic Dialogue & Reasoning
Pillar 02 · Clinical Reasoning

临床推理与主动对话Clinical AI & Active Reasoning

结合 DSM-5 / SCID 访谈标准与真实电子病历，研究能够主动提问、避免访谈漂移并具备精神病理学知识约束的诊断对话智能体。Developing clinically grounded dialogue systems guided by SCID protocols, EHRs, and psychiatric knowledge graphs for active interviewing.

代表成果：ICLR (Comorbidity Dialogues), ACL, NAACL
图 3. 心理大模型多维度评测基准 (PsyEval)Fig 3. Multi-Dimensional Psychological Evaluation Benchmark
Pillar 03 · Evaluation

心理大模型评测与基准Psychological Benchmarking

系统评估大语言模型在人类心理感知、认知推理与多轮共情交互中的表现，揭示并解决模型在情感智能上的碎片化现象。Systematically evaluating how foundation models perceive, reason about, and respond to human psychological states across perception, cognition, and interaction.

代表成果：npj Mental Health Research (PsyEval), arXiv 2026
图 4. 拟人化 AI 脆弱性治理与医疗伦理对齐体系Fig 4. Vulnerability Governance & Ethics Alignment
Pillar 04 · Safety & Ethics

伦理治理与安全护栏Ethical Governance & Safety

关注拟人化交互中的用户依赖与情感脆弱性，建立包含意图识别、五维危机响应与中国医疗伦理对齐的可靠安全边界。Addressing user vulnerability in anthropomorphic AI through medical ethics alignment pipelines (MedEthicEval) and crisis guardrails.

代表成果：Situating Vulnerability (2026), AAAI, NAACL
音乐与情感计算板块Music & Affective Generation

情绪音乐：从情感理解走向可控生成与个性化支持Affective Music: From Understanding to Controlled Generation

VALENCE–AROUSAL SPACE

用连续情感坐标描述音乐，实现可控生成与情感调节。 Describing Music via Continuous Valence–Arousal Coordinates.

效价（Valence）描述情绪正负倾向，唤醒度（Arousal）描述激活水平。连续坐标让“生成一首稍微更平静、更温暖的音乐”成为可能，并可结合可穿戴生理反馈闭环。 Valence describes positivity while arousal represents activation. Continuous coordinates enable precise affective generation and physiological feedback loops.

DATASET & BENCHMARK

MUSE-VA: Affective Music Dataset

面向音乐情感计算的合成多模态数据集，包含 6,254 首完整音乐、321.1 小时音频及 12 类离散情感标签与音乐—图像配对。A multimodal dataset for music emotion recognition containing 6,254 tracks, 321.1 audio hours, and 12 discrete emotion categories.
